Who We Are

Engineering & art,
together.

We're a couple of engineers and artists — one a painter, both tinkerers — who spent years designing, 3D printing, and building things from the ground up just for the joy of it.

Between us we bring together mechanical engineering, fine art painting, 3D fabrication, and client-facing work. We know how to design something beautiful and build it so it actually works.

Vitrine Taj is where all of that comes together — the intersection of engineering and art, applied to the most overlooked real estate in retail: the shop window.

The Moment Everything Changed

Travelling
Europe,
something
stopped us.

From Paris to Florence, Amsterdam to Lisbon — everywhere we went, small independent boutiques had these incredible, living window displays that made you feel something before you stepped inside.

Wonder. Curiosity. That irresistible pull to get closer. Sometimes you'd stand for several minutes just to figure out how it worked.

We came home and couldn't stop thinking: why don't shops here feel like that? The craft, the storytelling, the theatre of a beautiful window — it seemed like a lost art.

So we decided to revive it. Vitrine Taj exists to bring that magic back — one window at a time.

What We Believe

Things we refuse to
compromise on.

01
Everything custom. Always.
Every display is designed and built from scratch. We will never sell you a template or a pre-made prop. That's not a selling point — it's the entire point.
02
The window is a stage.
We treat every window like a theatre set — every element serves the story. Colour, light, motion, texture: nothing is accidental.
03
Small shops deserve great design.
The best boutiques in Paris don't have million-dollar budgets — they have passion and craft. We exist to serve that kind of shop.
04
Curiosity is the point.
The best displays make people stop, tilt their head, and wonder. We design for that moment of pause. That's what we're chasing every time.
